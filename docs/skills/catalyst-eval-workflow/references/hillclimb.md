# hillclimb: bounded comparison over a trusted frozen eval

This mode describes a future authorized run. Do not start it while preparing or
reviewing this skill. A valid completed live baseline and enough independent truth
must exist for the metric being optimized. Engineering/structural improvement and
enterprise semantic improvement are separate objectives.

1. Require a runnable existing eval, objective-qualified eval-audit, frozen inputs/grading,
   comparable Parent/control, explicit change allowlist and actor identity. Reuse
   HostAdapter and original Provider/P0/Runtime/Evaluation/R4; inspect their actual
   current paths before selecting an entry. If no qualified existing entry is found,
   report the seam missing; do not invent one or resurrect an exhausted Host root.
2. Freeze a plan: target metric, guardrails, case set, repetitions/split, effect worth
   acting on, permitted files, proposal actor, how changes materialize/rollback,
   rounds and costs for proposal+Subject+decision+Judge if any. Normal use does not
   need a manually entered experiment cap; this multi-run experiment does.
3. Keep independent held-out material outside optimizer access. Use structurally
   enforced isolation when claiming blind held-out evaluation. Training feedback
   can include approved failed behaviours, not withheld answers. With too few cases
   or no holdout, label evidence developmental/directional, not generalized gain.
4. In a fresh candidate, the declared proposal actor makes one causal hypothesis
   per round. For Catalyst-autonomous experiments only its approved LLM may propose
   or revise the method; Codex may supervise wiring but cannot substitute authored
   semantic improvements. Parent, evaluator and Owner facts are immutable.
5. Host verifies path/hash/size/file protection and materializes a versioned candidate;
   it must not auto-repair an invalid model proposal. Execute through the same entry
   with approved runtime profile and fresh durable ledger. Preserve natural tool
   choice. No implicit retry, model switch or budget extension.
6. Independently evaluate the exact resulting artifacts. Classify proposal, execution,
   infrastructure, evaluation and task failure separately. Record source-supported
   information lost as well as structural improvements; fewer Claims or more UNKNOWN
   does not by itself prove progress. Compare with a same-environment control when
   versions/model/serving state may have drifted.
7. Let the authorized decision actor choose NEXT/REVERT/STOP within the plan.
   Experiment KEEP is not production Reference promotion or Wiki admission. If the
   grader needs repair, halt this comparison and create a new measurement version
   plus derived rescoring; retain every original grade and account for all expense.
8. Stop on unauthorized drift, uncertain dispatch, invalid isolation, missing truth
   for the claimed objective, regression gate, completed plan, or explicit Owner stop.
   Do not retry/reopen historical runs, extend the plan, merge, activate or publish.

Deliver independent autonomous-loop, proposal-contract, Subject-execution,
feedback-consumption, structural-change and semantic-change verdicts. Include exact
commit/tree/patch, immutable receipts, compare table, measured costs and rollback.
This skill cannot make a weak evaluator trustworthy or guarantee LLM convergence.

## Penguin-derived experiment discipline

Each bounded attempt starts from an identified Reference and has a restorable
snapshot. Keep the frozen case and scoring identities separate from the candidate;
do not let the optimizer access private rubrics or held-out answers. Evaluate the
complete declared comparison before deciding to keep or revert. A rejected candidate
does not become the starting point for the next proposal. If a concurrent change or
an uncertain dispatch prevents a safe comparison, stop and preserve the evidence.
These are adapted method instructions; they do not create an autonomous executor or
authorize a model call or production promotion.

## Objective-specific admission
An audit is qualified for a named objective when all blockers that could invalidate
that objective's measurement are resolved. Engineering/structure-only comparison
may accept NOT_EVALUATED semantic quality if it is explicitly outside the objective,
with valid structure controls and no corresponding regression claim. Semantic
optimization additionally requires independent confirmed truth or a calibrated
approved semantic grader, enough representative evidence, and a comparable valid
baseline. PASS_WITH_LIMITS never silently qualifies a broader objective. Missing
measurement facts remain UNPROVEN; minor unrelated audit findings do not block all
objectives. Live authorization and protected-effect gates are separate in every case.
